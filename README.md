## 👋 About

Network engineering student building hands-on labs in **Cisco Modeling Labs (CML)**, **Cisco Packet Tracer**, and virtual appliances. Each project documents its design, configuration, verification evidence, and what was and was not tested.

---

## 🎯 Focus areas

| Area | What I work with |
|---|---|
| 🔀 **Routing and switching** | OSPF, HSRP and VRRP, STP, LACP, VLANs, and inter-VLAN routing |
| 🌐 **WAN and VPN** | MPLS L3VPN, GRE, IPsec with IKEv2, and SD-WAN-style failover with IP SLA |
| 🛡️ **Network security** | Zone-based firewalls, FortiGate NGFW, Cisco ISE with 802.1X, and AAA |
| 🤖 **Automation and monitoring** | Ansible, VyOS, PRTG, and Nautobot IPAM |
| 📞 **Voice** | SIP signaling with VitalPBX |

## 🧾 How I document every lab

| Part | What you will find |
|---|---|
| **Design** | The topology, the addressing plan, and the reasons behind the choices |
| **Configuration** | The key configuration, with secrets removed |
| **Verification evidence** | Numbered screenshots of the commands and tests that prove it works |
| **What was and was not tested** | Test results, a troubleshooting log, and known limitations |
